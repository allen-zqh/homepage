Assistant Professor

I am an Assistant Professor at the Shanghai International College of Intellectual Property (SICIP), Tongji University. I received my Ph.D. in Innovation Management from the Department of Technology Management for Innovation (TMI), The University of Tokyo, under the supervision of Prof. Kazuyuki Motohashi. My research lies at the intersection of innovation management, intellectual property, and science and technology policy. I examine the institutional settings, policy arrangements, and behavioral mechanisms involved in the creation, protection, diffusion, and value realization of knowledge and technology, as well as the measurement and evaluation of innovation and its broader economic and social implications.

Methodologically, I use natural language processing (NLP), large language models (LLMs), spatial analysis, and geographic information systems (GIS) to analyze patent, firm, and regional data, complemented by the automated collection of unstructured text from corporate websites and other online sources. My research seeks to identify and measure technological novelty, knowledge flows, and innovation value, providing empirical evidence for intellectual property research and science and technology policy evaluation.
